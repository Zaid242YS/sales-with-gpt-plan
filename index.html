A six-minute demonstration that respects the buyer

  1. Ask: “How do you currently prepare this decision, and what takes the most time?”
  2. Enter a few buyer-provided figures and show the resulting cost or enrollment calculation.
  3. Change one assumption. Show how the decision changes.
  4. Open the three choices. Let the buyer select an option.
  5. Export a customer proposal or internal report. Show that internal notes are excluded from the customer document.
  6. Explain the $550 scope, browser-local storage and backup process. Ask whether this solves a problem worth paying to address.

Hosting cost: Vercel’s Hobby plan is restricted to personal, non-commercial use. Use a plan permitted for commercial work, with the buyer paying hosting directly. See Vercel plan terms and GitHub deployment documentation (reviewed October 16, 2026).

Configuration is local: Settings entered in the running app belong to that browser. A buyer opens the app and enters their settings, or privately imports their app backup. Those settings do not automatically become the configuration for every visitor to the Vercel URL. For a broadly distributed branded default, edit the source deliberately and keep private data out of it.

Send the exported customer proposal to customers. Presentation mode is for an owner-led demo; it is not a secure customer portal. For a remotely shared editable customer portal or synchronized staff access, budget a separate backend project.

Buyer acceptance checklist

  • All three options calculate correctly against a manually checked buyer example.
  • Business identity, currency, scope and terms are correct.
  • Saved records reopen after refresh; editing and duplication work.
  • Remove sample data removes flagged samples while preserving real records.
  • Clear all data leaves no sample values or records after refresh.
  • Customer reports contain no internal cost or note fields.
  • Print / Save PDF works in the buyer’s browser; choose Save as PDF in the print dialog.
  • Backup export and restore work, and the buyer understands that there is no cloud synchronization.
  • Mobile display and the buyer’s actual desktop browser are checked before acceptance.
